Kliko 1.13 introduces My Things: save your car, devices and sizes, and Kliko checks listings against them.

🧩 My Things
• In your profile: up to 5 cars with wheel specs, your iPhone, laptop, monitor and TV, your height and measurements.
• Tires, wheels, phone cases, laptop bags, clothing and shoes: a badge on the listing shows whether the item fits, based on the seller’s description. In the feed, tap “Fits mine”.
• Everything stays on your phone, and one tap deletes it all. No account needed.

🏡 My home, computer and wardrobe
• My home: bed, windows, TV, lamps, doors and elevator — check whether a purchase will fit, and see matching listings under each section.
• My computer: a map of your PC or laptop, specs from a screenshot, and upgrade tips with offers from Kliko listings.
• My wardrobe: your clothes and outfits, tips on what goes with what, and items in your size.
• A “For you” shelf on the Home tab, and items bought with Safe deal go to My Things in one tap.

📐 See it for real
• “Will it fit?”: place the real dimensions of a sofa, wardrobe or TV from the listing in your room, or a laptop or monitor right on your desk.
• The AR ruler measures a nook or doorway and compares it with the item: room to spare, a tight fit or too small.
• “Try on live”: glasses, hats, jewelry and watches appear on you in the camera, and you can save the shot to Photos.

🚗 Tires and wheels
• “Will they fit?”: tires and wheels are checked against your car — bolt pattern, offset, center bore and diameter.
• The tire calculator shows how your speedometer reading and ground clearance will change.
• The camera reads tire and wheel markings, including when you’re selling them.

🏷️ Listings
• At the bottom: “Buy safely” (where Safe deal is available), “Offer” and “Contact” — chat, call or WhatsApp.
• “Next listing” — keep browsing without going back to the feed.
• “Listing breakdown” under the price and a “Below market” tag — a hint, not an inspection or a guarantee.

📦 Profile and deals
• “Needs your attention” on your profile shows deals where it’s your move.
• Deals are split into 4 steps marked “Your move”, and delivery stages show on your Lock Screen.
• Listing stats for 7 and 30 days, and all invoices and documents on one screen with search.
• My listings: clear dates and short statuses.

📸 Selling
• Quick takes about 30 seconds, Detailed goes step by step, and the category is picked from the title.
• A built-in camera with a frame that shows the photo the way buyers will see it.
• Studio puts your item on a clean background, and the price hint compares it with similar listings on Kliko.
• Clothing and shoe sizes in the system you prefer: EU, RU, US, UK, INT or cm. Dimensions are optional, with ready-made templates.

💬 Chat and iPhone
• Kliko AI suggests three replies in chat.
• Offline, your chats, deals and listings stay visible, and messages send automatically once you’re back online.
• A Kliko widget for your Home Screen, plus quick menus when you touch and hold a listing.

⚡ Faster and easier
• The app opens faster, and Home and the other tabs stay smooth.
• A new Home tab: categories in a grid and listings right away. Categories have new icons, and Animals is now Pets.
• Sign in with Google, and the SMS code fills in automatically when you sign up.
• Easier photo search: results widen from your city to all of Kazakhstan.
